# Privacy Policy — Twitch Short Bot

**Last updated: 25 September 2026**

1. **Data we collect.** TikTok account identifiers (such as `open_id` and display
   name) and OAuth access/refresh tokens; the video files and transcripts you process;
   and basic operational logs.

2. **Purpose.** This data is used only to authenticate your account and to
   upload/publish your own videos to your own TikTok account.

3. **Storage.** Data is stored on a self-hosted server controlled by the operator.
   Access tokens are stored locally and are never sold or shared with third parties.

4. **Third parties.** Data is transmitted to the TikTok API solely to perform the
   uploads you request. No data is sold and no data is used for advertising.

5. **Retention and deletion.** Tokens and media are retained until you delete them or
   revoke access. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. **Children.** The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not
   knowingly collect data from them.

7. **Contact.** For any question regarding this policy or to request deletion:
   [tiktokcontact.w27nn@passmail.net](mailto:tiktokcontact.w27nn@passmail.net)
