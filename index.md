# Twitch Short Bot

**Turn long Twitch recordings into ready-to-post vertical videos for TikTok.**

Twitch Short Bot is a web application for streamers and content creators. It takes
your Twitch video-on-demand (VOD) recordings, finds the most engaging moments, and
renders them into short, vertical (9:16) videos with burned-in subtitles. You review
each clip on the website and upload it to your TikTok account with one click.

## How it works

1. **Add a recording** — import your Twitch VOD into the application.
2. **Automatic analysis** — the audio is transcribed and the most engaging moments are detected.
3. **Vertical rendering** — the selected moments are reformatted to 9:16 with your webcam overlay and styled subtitles.
4. **Review** — preview each generated clip in the web interface, edit the title, and approve or reject it.
5. **Publish** — approved clips are uploaded to your TikTok inbox as drafts, ready to post.

## Features

- Automatic highlight detection from long recordings
- 9:16 vertical reformatting with webcam overlay
- Burned-in, styled subtitles
- Web review interface to approve, edit or reject each clip
- One-click upload to TikTok
- Optional daily publishing schedule

## TikTok integration

- **Login Kit — `user.info.basic`** — lets you sign in with TikTok and links your uploads to the correct account.
- **Content Posting API — `video.upload`** — uploads approved clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is my content published automatically?**
Clips are uploaded to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You keep the final decision and post
them from the TikTok app, or approve them for scheduled publishing.

**Which videos can I use?**
Only recordings you own or are authorized to use.

**How is my data handled?**
Processing runs on our own servers and only handles your own content. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
