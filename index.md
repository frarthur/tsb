# Twitch Short Bot

**Twitch Short Bot** is a personal content-automation tool that converts the
operator's own Twitch video-on-demand (VOD) recordings into short, vertical (9:16)
videos with subtitles, and uploads or publishes them to the operator's own TikTok
account.

- It processes only the operator's **own** content.
- Videos are uploaded to the operator's TikTok **inbox as drafts**, or published
  directly to the operator's own profile after the operator explicitly approves
  each clip.
- It is a single-user tool, not a public or multi-user service.
